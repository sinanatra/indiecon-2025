This toolkit is part of Reclaiming the night sky, developed for indiecon 2025. It examines how Internet infrastructure, particularly satellite networks such as Starlink, is changing our night sky and digital environment. It allows to print a series of A4 sheets to draw new fictional constellations by connecting Starlink satellites and stars. It's a simple way to reflect on how technology is transforming what we see above us and how we might imagine it differently.
